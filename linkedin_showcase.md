> [!TIP]
> **Converting WebP to Video for LinkedIn:**
> Since LinkedIn does not natively accept WebP animations directly in the "Video" upload slot:
> 1. You can upload the WebP file directly as an **Image** (it will play as an animated GIF in the feed).
> 2. Alternatively, convert the `.webp` file to `.mp4` using a free tool like Handbrake, ffmpeg, or an online converter. This will allow you to post it as a high-quality video with a voice-over track.
